Second Korean Wars (제 2차 한국전쟁) is a first-person story game that does not look at a war from one side only.

A South Korean special forces sergeant on his way home on leave, a North Korean soldier following his orders, and Ri Chung-seong. You move between three people who stand in very different places, and live through the moment the war begins and what comes after.

■ Features
• A first-person story: cutscenes, dialogue, and firefights you fight yourself
• One story followed from both sides, South and North
• Korean and English (switch any time in Settings)
• Touch controls for phones
• Plays offline: no account, no ads, no in-app purchases, no data collection
• New stories will keep being added

■ Please note
• The game shows gun combat, deaths and an execution scene in a war setting. Swearing is censored. It is intended for adult players.
• All characters and events are fictional.

■ Requirements
• Android 12 or newer
• A graphics chip with WebGPU support (recent Qualcomm Adreno or ARM Mali)
• An up-to-date "Android System WebView" (update it in the Play Store)
• The graphics preset (LOW, MEDIUM, HIGH) is chosen automatically for your device and can be changed in Settings
